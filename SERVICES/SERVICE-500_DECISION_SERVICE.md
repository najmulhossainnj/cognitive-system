# Purpose

The Decision Service provides the implementation of the Decision Capability for the Cognitive Operating System.

It evaluates candidate alternatives generated by the Planning Capability, coordinates specialized decision services, integrates decision evidence, and selects the most appropriate course of action according to system objectives, policies, and risk constraints.

Unlike the Planning Service, which generates possible plans, the Decision Service is responsible for selecting the preferred alternative.

The service implements the Decision Capability defined in **CORE-140 — Decision Capability**.

---

This specification defines:

- Decision orchestration
- Alternative evaluation
- Decision strategy selection
- Utility evaluation coordination
- Policy compliance coordination
- Risk evaluation coordination
- Decision explanation
- Decision traceability
- Public interfaces
- Configuration
- Events
- Telemetry

This specification does not define:

- Plan generation
- Logical reasoning
- Plan execution
- Learning
- World modelling

These responsibilities belong to other capabilities.

---

# Architectural Philosophy

The Decision Service answers:

> **"Which available alternative should be selected?"**

It does not generate alternatives.

It evaluates alternatives generated by the Planning Capability and selects the most appropriate decision using specialized decision services.

Planning proposes.

Decision selects.

Execution acts.

---

# Responsibilities

The Decision Service shall:

- receive candidate alternatives
- coordinate decision evaluation
- invoke specialized decision services
- integrate evaluation results
- rank alternatives
- select a preferred decision
- explain decisions
- maintain decision traceability

The service shall not:

- generate plans
- perform reasoning
- execute decisions
- learn policies
- modify the World Model

# Internal Components

## Decision Coordinator

Coordinates the complete decision lifecycle.

Responsibilities include:

- evaluation scheduling
- service coordination
- decision orchestration
- lifecycle management

---

## Alternative Manager

Maintains candidate alternatives.

Each alternative includes:

- identifier
- originating planner
- assumptions
- expected outcome
- metadata

---

## Strategy Selector

Determines which evaluation services should participate.

Representative services include:

- Utility Decision Service
- Policy Engine Service
- Risk Assessment Service

Selection policies are configurable.

---

## Evaluation Integrator

Combines evaluation results into a unified decision model.

Integration may consider:

- utility scores
- policy compliance
- risk exposure
- confidence
- priorities

Integration strategies remain implementation independent.

---

## Decision Repository

Maintains decision history.

Representative information includes:

- selected alternative
- rejected alternatives
- evaluation results
- rationale
- timestamps

---

## Explanation Manager

Produces implementation-independent explanations.

Example explanations include:

- decision rationale
- utility contribution
- policy constraints
- risk considerations
- evaluation summary

---

## Decision Trace Manager

Maintains full traceability.

Traceability includes:

- originating goal
- candidate plans
- evaluation services
- final decision
- execution identifier

# Summary

The Decision Service provides the orchestration layer for decision making within the Cognitive Operating System. By coordinating utility evaluation, policy compliance, and risk assessment through specialized decision services, it transforms candidate alternatives into justified, traceable, and explainable decisions. This separation between planning, evaluation, and execution establishes a modular, extensible, and implementation-independent decision architecture that remains consistent with the overall capability-oriented design of the Cognitive Operating System.
